# Lore and Worldbuilding

After the creation of Simple Domain and the subsequent foundation of the New Shadow Style back in the Heian Era, it accumulated power over the centuries. The binding vow which was more akin to a spell was used much more aggressively to gain wealth and control in the country. Unlike the original JJK timeline, where typically it only allowed the owner to absorb the lifespan of the disciples and force them to commit to any call to action, here it imposed a far larger potential control of the student. Jujutsu did not fall off in the public counciousness and instead became a product. The Jujutsu Society (which was mingled with the government of Japan) and the New Shadow Group (the former New Shadow Style School that evolved into a corporation) progressed Sorcery and Cursed Energy as a consumer's product over the last 3 centuries, perfecting the art of Cursed Tools/Objects mass production and developing the capability to engrave the capability to control Cursed Energy and Cursed Techniques into brains via refined surgery. As the technology advanced, they figured out how to merge Cursed Tools into the users, offering greater control, mastery and cabalities. The New Shadow Group figured out a way to integrate binding vows into the merged Cursed Tools which would impact the host. While these were not as strong as binding vows directly placed upon the host, they made them more inclined to rely on the corporation and even join their forces if such a need be. The New Shadow Group and the Jujutsu Society are in a constant silent war over who can accumulate more power and control over the country, and because of this, there is almost no person in Japan who does not possess the capability to control their CE. Natural born sorcerers with techniques are still at a better baseline since they don't have to get any involvement with either of the sides. Information on Cursed Energy is very easily found since it's public knowledge, so self-taught sorcerers are as common as official ranking ones as of 2020, when the events of the story are set.
